Help and support

Updated: 2026-11-08

New Note

Choose a date and tap New Note. The toolbar below the text lets you add a photo, a recording or a checklist.

Backup & Migration

In Settings, open data management, then Backup & Migration to export a copy. Keep the password somewhere safe if you encrypt the file.

Recording Transcript

The app requests camera or microphone access only when you use photo capture, scanning or recording. You choose photos and files through system pickers. Declining these permissions does not prevent you from writing text notes. Transcription requires system speech-recognition permission and uses on-device recognition only. If the device or language is unsupported, the recording is kept and transcription is reported as unavailable. The app does not fall back to cloud recognition. You can revoke permissions in system settings.

Recently Deleted

Open Recently Deleted in data settings to restore a note. After permanent deletion, you need a backup made before the note was deleted.
